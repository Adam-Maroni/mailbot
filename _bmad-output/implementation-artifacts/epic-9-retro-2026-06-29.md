## 2. The L1 / L2 / L3 Framework — The Key Insight From This Retro

A single insight reshaped the retrospective discussion. There are **three layers of "done"** for any MailBot epic, and Epic 9 only crossed the first two:

| Layer | Meaning | Epic 9 status |
|---|---|---|
| **L1: Code-complete** | All ACs implemented, gates green, tests passing | ✅ 12/12 stories done |
| **L2: Self-verified** | Tests cover the contract; synthetic fixtures exercise happy path + edges | ✅ +488 net tests, ~93% CR applied-rate |
| **L3: Live-validated** | Real Hermes session, real Anthropic API, real production emails, real Adam-observable outcome | ❌ **Not yet** — Phase 3.5 walks deferred, ~$11-14 real-spend pending |

**Why L1+L2 ≠ L3 for Epic 9 specifically:**

1. **The benchmark harness is a measurement instrument.** Until pointed at real corpus + real models, the measurement's validity itself is unknown. Krippendorff α (Story 9-7) has never been computed on real evaluator output. If α<0.6 on real 20-anchor walk, done-flip clause 9 **BLOCKS** and we discover the eval pipeline is single-point-of-failure.

2. **`policy.yaml` still has hypothesis annotations, not evidence.** Every Opus task has a `# DEMOTION HYPOTHESIS: ...` comment. Epic 9's purpose was to convert at least one into a measured verdict. **No hypothesis converted yet.** The router is making the same choices it was making before Epic 9 shipped.

3. **`/model` has never been invoked from Discord.** MCP verbs work in tests; SKILL.md docs are correct (Story 9-10 enforces); but Hermes Discord runtime slash-command registration is **unowned architectural debt** (§ 3.4 of tranche retro). `/model` ships as MCP-dispatchable-via-prompt; the Discord-Portal-API integration that wires it to actual chat commands does not exist.

4. **The product is not deployed.** CP-1 is the final ship gate (Adam-decided 2026-06-04). Until VPS deploy + first real ingest tick, L3 validation isn't possible at all.

**The L1/L2/L3 vocabulary becomes a durable mental model** (action item B3). Future epic-close conversations: first question is "done at which layer?"

## 6. What Didn't Go Well

1. **The biggest gap, named directly:** Epic 9 built a measurement instrument and never measured anything with it. +488 tests, ~$0 real Anthropic spend, zero real production emails through the new surface. The whole epic was L1+L2 work. Option A close honors the gap; B1/B5 below close it.

2. **Autonomous-epic-run / autonomous-story-run cadence makes the L3 gap structural** — Phase 3.5 walks only fire on epic done-flip, not on story close. Tranche-close (no Phase 3.5) → Epic-close-on-Option-A (Phase 3.5 also doesn't fire) means a 12-story epic shipped without a single live walk for 10 of the 12 stories. Worth naming as a pattern; doesn't change the cadence (the cadence is right for L1+L2 stories) but it explains why Epic 9.5 is necessary.

3. **Story 9-5 AC-15 amendment shifted the benchmark's MEANING.** Original contract: "Adam-by-hand labels because if labels ARE pipeline output, the scorer compares pipeline-to-itself." The 2026-06-27 amendment ("forget about the benchmark, we will move with LLM recommendations") made the live co-pilot session ship. But Story 9-7's scorer now measures **pipeline-LLM-vs-labeler-LLM agreement, NOT pipeline-LLM-vs-Adam-judgment.** Documented in run-flags, surfaced here as B4 to prevent over-interpretation of future DEMOTE/PROMOTE verdicts.

4. **Hermes Discord runtime registration was unowned debt for 2 weeks.** Hit three times across the `/model` tranche (Stories 9-3, 9-4, 9-10 all discharged ACs because of it). We kept discharging ACs around the missing surface instead of scoping the missing surface. D3 above closes this — it becomes Epic 9.5 AC-9.

## 11. Closing Note

Epic 9 was the largest epic by tests (+488 net), by surface area (manual override + benchmark harness as one architectural mandate), and by stretch (12 stories across two distinct tranches with a 14-day park between them). It shipped clean at L2.

The honest framing — and the most important takeaway from this retro — is that **L2 isn't L3**. The benchmark machinery exists. The `/model` knob exists. The audit vocabulary, the cohort_key, the Krippendorff α, the Wilson CIs, the Pareto frontier, the DEMOTE/PROMOTE verdict engine — all real, all tested, all unused against reality.

Epic 9.5 closes that gap. The `policy.yaml` v0→v1 bump (clause 11) is the load-bearing moment when Epic 9's purpose is fulfilled: when at least one Opus-vs-Haiku hypothesis becomes a measured verdict cited to a real `benchmark_runs.run_id`, OR Adam signs off in writing that no change is warranted with the evidence pointing the way.

The L1/L2/L3 vocabulary stays with us. Next epic close, the first question is: "done at which layer?"
